Drawings – Roslagsgatan 35 (Vilan 17)

This page contains 193 drawings for Roslagsgatan 35, which is the property Vilan 17. The drawings come from the City of Stockholm's building permit archive and have Swedish titles.

The cooperative consists of two properties: Roslagsgatan 37 is Vilan 16 and Roslagsgatan 35 is Vilan 17. This page covers Roslagsgatan 35 (Vilan 17). The drawings for Roslagsgatan 37 (Vilan 16) are on a separate page. TIF and PDF files are the originals from the archive; PNG and JPG files are viewing copies. Click a thumbnail to view the drawing.
