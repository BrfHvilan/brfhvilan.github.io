News

News and information from the board. Most recent first.

  • 20 October 2026

    Long-term radon measurement

    In the spring, the board carried out a short-term radon measurement in order to avoid a possible fine. A long-term measurement is now required. The board will distribute radon detectors in the building during November; the measurement will run for at least three months.

  • 19 October 2026

    Information about the mandatory ventilation inspection (OVK)

    The cooperative's OVK inspection has been carried out in most of the apartments. The board is now following up the results together with the contractor Hemkomfort. Remedial work in the apartments that received remarks began on 20 October. Apartments that could not be inspected will at the same time be notified of a new inspection time.

    For the cooperative to obtain an approved OVK report, it is important that all apartments are inspected. Questions are answered by the board at styrelsenhvilan@gmail.com.
