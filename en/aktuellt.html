News

News and information from the board. Most recent first.

  • 27 October 2026

    New internet subscription – each household orders a new router

    The cooperative will shortly be changing its internet subscription. In connection with the change, each household will need to order a new router via My Pages (Mina sidor) at Telia. The board will provide more information about when the change will take place. Questions are answered by the board at styrelsenhvilan@gmail.com.

  • 24 October 2026

    Autumn clean-up day on Sunday 8 November

    On Sunday 8 November it is time for the cooperative's autumn clean-up. We will meet in the courtyard at 11:00 and work together to get the courtyard, the bicycle racks and the shared spaces ready for winter. The cooperative will provide coffee and snacks, and as usual we will finish with a shared barbecue for anyone who would like to stay on. All members are very welcome – the more of us there are, the quicker it goes.

  • 20 October 2026

    Long-term radon measurement

    As part of the cooperative's monitoring of the indoor environment, and to meet the authorities' measurement requirements, a short-term radon measurement was carried out in the spring. A long-term measurement now follows: the board will distribute radon detectors to the apartments concerned during November, and the measurement will run for at least three months over the winter. Questions are answered by the board at styrelsenhvilan@gmail.com.

  • 19 October 2026, updated 23 October 2026

    Information about the mandatory ventilation inspection (OVK)

    The cooperative's OVK inspection has been carried out in most of the apartments. The board is now following up the results together with the contractor Hemkomfort. Remedial work in the apartments that received remarks began on 20 October. Apartments that could not be inspected will at the same time be notified of a new inspection time. Defects relating to the cooperative's ducts and fans will be remedied by the cooperative; defects caused by installations in members' own apartments, such as incorrectly connected kitchen extractor fans, must be remedied by the member.

    For the cooperative to obtain an approved OVK report, it is important that all apartments are inspected. Questions are answered by the board at styrelsenhvilan@gmail.com.
