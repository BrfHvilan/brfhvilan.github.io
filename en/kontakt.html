Emergencies

Water leak: turn off the water if it is safe to do so, start mopping up and call TEKAB on 08-640 10 00; outside office hours, use the emergency number on the notice board in the stairwell.

Lift stuck: use the emergency telephone in the lift. It goes directly to the lift company's emergency service.

Fire: call 112.

Reporting faults

Non-urgent faults in shared spaces, such as broken lighting, doors, locks or the laundry room machines, should be reported to TEKAB on 08-640 10 00 during office hours. Members are responsible for faults in their own apartment, see the For members page. How the cooperative handles personal data

The cooperative processes members' names, addresses, apartment numbers and contact details, as well as information about ownership shares, pledges and transfers, in order to fulfil its obligations under the Swedish Housing Cooperatives Act (bostadsrättslagen) – the register of members and the apartment register – and to be able to inform members. The data is kept for as long as you are a member; financial data is then kept for seven years under the Swedish Bookkeeping Act, and data in the apartment register for as long as the law requires. Kanold Redovisning AB processes the data on the cooperative's behalf. The Facebook group is voluntary and is run on Facebook's terms. You have the right to know what data the cooperative holds about you and to have incorrect data corrected: contact the board at styrelsenhvilan@gmail.com.
