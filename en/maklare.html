Transfer fee
Under section 4 of the statutes: 2.5% of the price base amount (SEK 1,480 in 2026), paid by the buyer.
Pledging fee
Under section 4 of the statutes: 1% of the price base amount (SEK 592 in 2026), paid by the pledgor.
Subletting
Requires the board's written consent. Consent is normally given for a fixed period, usually one year at a time, for acceptable reasons such as work or studies elsewhere, and each application is assessed individually. A refusal can be referred to the Rent Tribunal (hyresnämnden). Fee according to the statutes.
Legal entities
As a general rule, the cooperative does not grant membership to legal entities acquiring a residential apartment; statutory exceptions may apply.
Joint ownership
Accepted. Membership is assessed individually in accordance with the law and the statutes.

Finances at a glance

Annual fee
SEK 736 per sq m per year in 2025 (10% increase on 1 April 2025); approximately SEK 818 per sq m in 2026 with the full-year effect of the 2025 and 2026 increases. Average monthly fee in 2026 approximately SEK 4,100.
Loans
SEK 24,964,000 (October 2026), equivalent to approximately SEK 6,700 per sq m of residential and commercial area. Variable interest rate, 2.34% on average.
Loan-to-value ratio
16% of the tax assessment value (SEK 160 million).
Equity ratio
61% (31 December 2025).
Commercial premises
Three let premises generating approximately SEK 790,000 per year, 21% of income.
Maintenance plan
50-year maintenance plan drawn up in 2026 by Sustend.
Energy class
C (energy performance certificate valid until May 2027).
Fee for 2027
The board will set the budget and fee for 2027 on 19 November 2026; the decision will be published under News.

From the 2026 financial year, the cooperative applies component depreciation under K3, with a component breakdown prepared by Sustend. Depreciation therefore increases from approximately SEK 276,000 to approximately SEK 834,000 per year. This reduces the reported result in the annual reports for 2026 onwards but affects neither the cooperative's cash flow nor the fees.
